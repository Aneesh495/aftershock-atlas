# ADR 004: Keep current geography distinct from historical identity

Status: accepted for the research preview.

## Context

Current ISO identifiers do not prove historical border stability or the date a state existed.

## Decision

Use current identifiers for the bounded panel, unknown validity intervals where unsupported, and additive conservation checks for any supplied crosswalk.

## Consequences and rejected alternative

No universal split/merge inference is fabricated. Historical crosswalk acceptance remains incomplete. Rates require recomputed matching denominators rather than weighted averages of rates.
