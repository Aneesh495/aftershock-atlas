# UK GDP calibration with dated release inputs

Forecast first-published quarterly UK real GDP growth. At each origin use the preceding quarter's archived national accounts release. That release is already available by the forecast day and predates the target quarter's first estimate. Inputs are within-edition quarterly growth rates. Compare against first estimates rather than growth after benchmarking revisions.

Keep all model development separated from the test period. The initial training source is the first estimate released on 11 November 2021. Within that archive, fit only 2001Q1 through 2018Q4, validate 2019Q1 through 2021Q3, and retain the first source's data definitions. Training and validation use a single edition known by the first forecast date. Forecasts use the new archive at each origin. Preserve the entire initial-week source and the origin-by-origin records.

Compare zero growth, persistence, four-quarter mean, same-quarter growth a year ago and ridge autoregression with four recent quarterly growth values. Freeze ridge penalties 1, 10 and 100. Select the stronger fixed baseline and ridge penalty by validation mean absolute error. Keep all fixed comparators, with calibration on the same archive availability schedule. Record the value of the target quarter that later appears in the same quarter's national accounts publication as a revision-sensitivity outcome.

Calibrate nominal 90% point-centered intervals using finite-sample split-conformal absolute errors from the nine quarterly first estimates in 2021Q4 through 2023Q4. Freeze the multiplier before scoring 2024Q1 through 2025Q4. Publish the exact first-estimate-day residuals, interval width, empirical coverage, exact binomial uncertainty, mean absolute error, and interval score. An eight-quarter evaluation cannot establish nominal 90% coverage. Treat fewer than seven first estimates inside the interval as a practical failure, and publish calibration failures for every model. Select no models by test score. The 2024–25 targets were part of the earlier revision audit and some headline values were visible. They remain unused for selection, fitting and calibration in this forecast experiment.

This is a UK economic forecast. The mortality and life expectancy undercoverage in the multinational exercise stays visible and outside this calibration claim. Preserve outcomes where an original forecast cannot be validated against real releases.
