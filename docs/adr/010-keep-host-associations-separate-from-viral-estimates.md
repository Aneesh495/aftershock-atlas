# ADR 010: Keep host associations separate from viral estimates

Status: accepted for the research preview.

## Context

Host effects, viral lineages and national trajectories have different sampling units.

## Decision

Perform effect-allele and build checks on released HGI aggregates. Analyze one archived CDC weighted viral publication separately, with reliability suppression and overlapping-label warnings.

## Consequences and rejected alternative

Shared HGI samples prevent independent replication claims. Missing LD references limit locus independence. No ancestry inference or genetic explanation of national or ethnic outcomes is introduced.
