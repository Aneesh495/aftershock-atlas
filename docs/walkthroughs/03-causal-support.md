# Candidate causal support

`make causal` finds each country's first national full school-closure coding date. Dates form annual adoption cohorts. `group_time` uses the year before adoption as the baseline and compares change with not-yet-treated or never-treated countries that have both outcome values.

The 2020 cohort can produce descriptive contrasts, but the 2021 cohort has only two treated countries and fails the declared minimum of five. The function writes null effects with an explicit unsupported status. Pretrend diagnostics and pseudo-onsets remain in the artifact. Neither a computable contrast nor a non-significant pretrend test establishes identification.

Open the study register and choose S06. The table retains the two-country cells alongside computed-under-assumptions cells. The conclusion explains policy bundles, reverse causation and interference. No predicted no-shock curve is relabeled a causal estimate.
