# ADR 008: Distinguish predictive scenarios from causal effects

Status: accepted for the research preview.

## Context

A plausible pre-2020 band can be mistaken for an identified pandemic counterfactual.

## Decision

Label pre-period extrapolations as predictive no-shock scenarios. Candidate causal designs separately define adoption, eligible controls, pretrends, placebos and threats.

## Consequences and rejected alternative

Descriptive results remain useful even when identification fails. No non-significant pretrend test or fitted curve is promoted to causal evidence.
